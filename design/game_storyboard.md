# Project One Storyboard | Text-Based Adventure Game

## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**

The theme of my game is a medieval castle adventure game.

**Storyline:**

The game is set in the land of Pseudonia. Gilgamesh the Gruesome has been terrorizing local villagers for years from a castle he stole from the royal family when he devoured them in the middle of the night. Our hero Unther the Valiant has been contacted by several villagers for help ending the evil reign of Gilgamesh. Unther must enter Gilgamesh's castle and find items stolen from him by Gilgamesh's henchmen, before he can confront Gilgamesh. Only after obtaining these items, will Unther be able to defeat Gilgamesh. Gilgamesh has hidden the items in different rooms to prevent Unther from finding what he needs. Unther will need to be brave and search each room before trying to vanquish Gilgamesh. If Unther encounters Gilgamesh before finding all the items, Unther will become the next victim of Gilgamesh. The royal family built secret passages to make getting around the castle faster. The locations of these passages was only known by the royal family and the lone villager that designed them. Luckily for Unther, this lone villager is one of the people who reached out to Unther for help.

## Villain

The villain is Gilgamesh the Gruesome. He is a cyclops that has been terrorizing local villagers for years by eating their livestock and children that stray too far from their homes. His face and body are covered with scars from years of battles he has won, remnants of the victims he has ate are stuck in between his sharp teeth.
